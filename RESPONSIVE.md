# Responsive behavior

The portfolio uses a shared responsive stylesheet in `css/style.css`.

## Layout behavior

- Desktop keeps the two-column introduction, three-column content area, and horizontal footer.
- At widths below 900px, the introduction, about section, projects, timeline, and footer stack vertically in reading order.
- At widths below 600px, navigation links open from the hamburger menu, action buttons fill their grid columns, technology items wrap into a two-column list, and footer contact details stack vertically.
- Images and text are constrained to their containers so they do not create horizontal scrolling.

## Project carousel

The homepage carousel shows three cards on desktop, two cards on medium screens, and one card on phones. Arrow buttons, pointer dragging, and project links remain available at every breakpoint.

## Verification

Check the pages at approximately 375px, 600px, 768px, 900px, and 1280px wide. Confirm that the menu opens, carousel arrows and dragging work, all sections can be reached by scrolling, and the contact form fields remain usable without zooming.
